# Three-minute demo script

**Setup:** open the app at `/` in a full-size window. Click **Reset** so you start at baseline.

## 0:00 – 0:20 · Hook
"Cities are full of decisions that are hard to picture. *What if a neighborhood gave up 40% of its parking for green space?* This is Parallel — a fictional neighborhood where you can try it and see the result."
- Point at the 3D city: three surface lots, 144 spaces. Orbit once.

## 0:20 – 1:00 · The scripted scenario
- Click **Play 40% demo**. Narrate while stalls rise into planted strips and trees grow in across all three lots.
- Camera glides to the lot view. Right panel: **86 of 144 spaces retained**, **507.5 m² added green area**, **16 trees**, **320 m² added canopy at maturity**.
- "Every number comes from the same list of converted stall IDs that the 3D scene uses."

## 1:00 – 1:30 · The trade-off
- "86 spaces for 104 assumed cars — 18 have no space." Point to the amber markers and the **Parking pressure** card.
- Drag the slider back to 28% (the amber tick): "up to here, modelled demand is still met. Past it, every stall costs a car." The card pulses as it flips. (Demand is an illustrative assumption, not data.)

## 1:30 – 2:10 · The reveal
- Click **Play reveal** (or set 40%, choose **Compare** and drag by hand). The camera settles on the lots, then a divider sweeps across the city: **baseline on the left, 40% scenario on the right**, same camera, same lighting, same traffic.
- Drag the divider yourself. "Only the parking and the trees change — everything else is identical."
- Orbit or press **Top view** while comparing: both halves move together. Switch back to **After** — the scenario is untouched.

## 2:10 – 2:40 · Explore and honesty
- Drag the slider to ~70%, then 100% (while in Compare the right half updates live). Switch **Tree size shown** to *Newly planted* to show maturity is a modelling assumption.
- Open **Assumptions & limitations**: 12.5 m² per stall, 70% plantable, 30 m² per tree, 20 m² mature canopy, 104 assumed cars. "Fictional neighborhood, no real data, no temperature or health claims."

## 2:40 – 3:00 · Share
- Click **Share**, paste the link into a new tab: same scenario (and divider position) restored. "Change a planning decision, see an explainable, reproducible outcome."

**Reduced motion:** with the OS setting on, the demos jump between states (baseline → 28% → 40% → divider positions) instead of animating; the divider itself is always drag-controlled.
