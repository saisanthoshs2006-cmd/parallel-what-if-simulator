# Three-minute demo script

**Setup:** open the app at `/` in a full-size window. Click **Reset** so you start at baseline.

## 0:00 – 0:20 · Hook
"Cities are full of decisions that are hard to picture. *What if a neighborhood gave up 40% of its parking for green space?* This is Parallel — a fictional neighborhood where you can try it and see the result."
- Point at the 3D city: three surface lots, 144 spaces. Orbit once.

## 0:20 – 1:10 · The scripted scenario
- Click **Play 40% demo**. Narrate while stalls rise into planted strips and trees grow in across all three lots.
- Camera glides to the lot view. Right panel: **86 of 144 spaces retained**, **507.5 m² added green area**, **16 trees**, **320 m² added canopy at maturity**.
- "Every number comes from the same list of converted stall IDs that the 3D scene uses."

## 1:10 – 1:45 · Before / After
- Toggle **Before** → **After** (top of the scene). Lots flip between baseline and scenario instantly.
- Point to the stacked bar: baseline vs scenario parking.

## 1:45 – 2:25 · Explore
- Drag the slider to ~70%, then 100%. Show the response curve with the marker moving; note the steps (whole trees).
- Switch **Tree size shown** to *Newly planted* to show maturity is a modelling assumption, not today's canopy.

## 2:25 – 2:50 · Honesty
- Scroll the right panel to **Assumptions** and **Limitations**: 12.5 m² per stall, 70% plantable, 30 m² per tree, 20 m² mature canopy. "Fictional neighborhood, no real data, no temperature or health claims."

## 2:50 – 3:00 · Share
- Click **Share**, paste the link into a new tab: same scenario restored. "Change a planning decision, see an explainable, reproducible outcome."
