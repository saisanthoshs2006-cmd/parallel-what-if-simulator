# 90-second demo script

**Setup:** open the app, click **Reset** (baseline, overview camera).

## 0:00 – 0:15 · The parking problem
"Cities argue about parking without seeing it. This is a *fictional* neighborhood with three surface lots — 144 spaces. Every number you'll see comes from stated assumptions, not real data." Orbit once.

## 0:15 – 0:35 · Parking into greenery
Click **Play 40% demo**. "Converting 40% turns 58 stalls into planted strips: about 507 m² of green and 16 new trees." Point to the Green space panel.

## 0:35 – 0:55 · The trade-off
"But parking isn't free: we assume 104 cars need a space. 86 spaces remain, so 18 have none — shown in amber." Drag the slider to 28%: "Up to here, assumed demand still fits." Cross 29%: the card flips. "This is an illustration, not a prediction."

## 0:55 – 1:20 · The reveal
Set 40%, click **Play reveal** (or **Compare** and drag). "Left is today, right is the scenario — same camera, same lighting, same traffic. Only the parking and trees change." Drag the divider, orbit once.

## 1:20 – 1:30 · Why it's useful
"Parallel turns a planning argument into something you can see, tweak and share. Copy the link — it restores this exact view. Next step: real city data."
